PulsePlan privacy

Your data stays yours.

PulsePlan uses your information to create workouts, save progress, sync your account, and keep the app reliable. We do not sell your data or show ads.

What we save

  • Your email and account ID.
  • Your plan choices and optional movement note.
  • Workout progress, exercise results, notes, and reminders.
  • Crash and diagnostic information needed to fix the app.

Why we use it

  • To create and restore your workout plan.
  • To sync progress across your devices.
  • To send the reminder time you choose.
  • To find crashes and improve reliability.

Where it goes

Account, cloud-sync, and diagnostic services are provided by Google Firebase. Some data also stays on your device so the app can keep working between syncs.

Opening an exercise video search takes you to YouTube, where YouTube's own privacy terms apply.

How long we keep it

Account and workout data stays until you delete your account or ask us to delete it. Diagnostic data is retained only as needed to maintain PulsePlan and follows Firebase retention controls.

Delete your data

In PulsePlan, open Account & Privacy, then choose Delete account and data. This removes your Firebase account, cloud workout history, local app data, and scheduled reminder.

If you cannot use the app, submit a deletion request online. We verify the account email and complete valid requests within 7 days.
