PulsePlan account deletion

Delete your account and data.

You can delete everything inside PulsePlan or send a request if you no longer have access to the app.

Delete inside PulsePlan

  1. Open Account & Privacy.
  2. Select Delete account and data.
  3. Confirm your identity and deletion.

This is the fastest option and cannot be undone.

Request by email

Email us from the address used for your PulsePlan account. Keep the prepared subject and include the account email in your message.

Email deletion request →

What is deleted

We delete the Firebase authentication account and its cloud profile, plan settings, reminders, progress, exercise results, notes, and saved alternatives. Deleting through the app also clears local PulsePlan data and the scheduled reminder on that device.

What happens next

We may ask you to verify ownership of the account email. Valid requests are completed within 7 days. We will confirm when the deletion is finished.
